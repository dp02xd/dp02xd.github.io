HOW TO PLAY

MOVE • CAPTURE • FINISH

Roll a six to bring a pawn onto the board. Move your pawns around the track, capture opponents and get all four pawns home first.

ABOUT THE GAME

SIMPLE. OFFLINE. YOURS.

A lightweight classic Ludo game built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No account, server or internet connection is required.
